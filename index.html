Research profile

Learning and optimization for complex dynamical systems

My work combines reinforcement learning, optimal control, optimization, and data-driven modeling to address nonlinear systems with constraints, uncertainty, and safety requirements. Recent research spans safe actor–critic control, experience replay, LSTM-assisted uncertainty modeling, dynamic optimization, and physics-informed learning for industrial processes.

Research Projects

Safe Reinforcement Learning for Nonlinear Optimal Control

Experience Replay for Sample-Efficient Reinforcement Learning

Systematic study and methodological development of experience-replay strategies, including adaptive clustering and dual-buffer mechanisms for efficient and stable learning.

Experience ReplaySample EfficiencyClustering

LSTM-Assisted Bi-Level Control Under Uncertainty

Integration of recurrent learning and reinforcement learning within a bi-level optimal-control framework for nonlinear systems with uncertain dynamics.

Dynamic and Non-Stationary Optimization
